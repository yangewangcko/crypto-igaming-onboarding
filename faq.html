FAQ

Answers to the questions merchants ask most during onboarding.

How can I check a transaction's status and why it failed?

You can find this in two places — the Dashboard and the API.

Dashboard: open the Payments page and search by payment ID. Hover over the response code to see the status, and open the payment details page for the full breakdown.

API: the same response code is returned in the API response for every payment request.

How should my finance team reconcile with Checkout.com?

Follow our reconciliation guide, which walks through matching settlements and payouts against your processed payments.

How do I submit a support ticket?

Sign in to the Dashboard, click the question-mark icon in the upper-right corner, and select Request support.

How do I create API keys?

Sign in to the Dashboard, select the Developers icon in the top navigation bar, open the Keys tab, and select Create a new key. See the Developers documentation for details.

How do I set up single sign-on (SSO)?

Configure SSO for your team from the Dashboard — follow the SSO setup guide.

How can I view and update my business details?

You can view and update your business details directly in the Dashboard. See Manage business details.

How can I retrieve reports?

Checkout.com provides a range of reports to support your business operations and help you gain insight. You can retrieve them in four ways:

  • Generate and download reports in the Dashboard, on demand or on a schedule.
  • Retrieve reports programmatically using the Reports API.
  • Access reports over Secure File Transfer Protocol (SFTP).
  • Receive scheduled reports via webhooks.

See Retrieve reports for setup instructions.
