FAQ

Answers to the questions merchants ask most during onboarding.

How can I check a transaction's status and why it failed?

You can find this in two places — the Dashboard and the API.

Dashboard: open the Payments page and search by payment ID. Hover over the response code to see the status, and open the payment details page for the full breakdown.

API: the same response code is returned in the API response for every payment request.

How should my finance team reconcile with Checkout.com?

Follow our reconciliation guide, which walks through matching settlements and payouts against your processed payments.

How do I submit a support ticket?

Sign in to the Dashboard, click the question-mark icon in the upper-right corner, and select Request support.

How do I create API keys?

Sign in to the Dashboard, select Developers section in the left-side menu bar, click on Keys, and select Create a new key. See the Developers documentation for details.

How do I set up single sign-on (SSO)?

Configure SSO for your team from the Dashboard — follow the SSO setup guide.

How can I view and update my business details?

You can view and update your business details directly in the Dashboard. See Manage business details.

How can I retrieve reports?

Checkout.com provides a range of reports to support your business operations and help you gain insight. You can retrieve them in four ways:

  • Generate and download reports in the Dashboard, on demand or on a schedule.
  • Retrieve reports programmatically using the Reports API.
  • Access reports over Secure File Transfer Protocol (SFTP).
  • Receive scheduled reports via webhooks.

See Retrieve reports for setup instructions.

Can I set up recurring payments?

Yes. You can charge a customer on a recurring basis using their stored card details — see Recurring payments with stored card details.

What should be on my pre-go-live checklist?

Before you switch on live traffic, confirm your technical integration and configuration are production-ready:

  • Activate the Dashboard: ensure your Dashboard is activated and the right people on your team have access.
  • Swap API keys and endpoints: move your environment configuration from sandbox to production. Replace all test public and secret keys with the live production keys generated in the Dashboard.
  • Configure live webhooks: recreate all system-critical webhooks in your live environment. Make sure your server listens for live events such as card_verified, payment_approved, payment_declined, payment_captured, payment_refunded, and payment_voided, and that your live endpoints are secured and authenticated.
  • Enable 3D Secure (3DS) and fraud rules: review your 3DS rules in the Dashboard. Confirm Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) is enabled and correctly configured for regions where it is mandatory (such as Europe) to avoid immediate issuer declines.
  • Configure reporting methods: set up your reporting channels (Dashboard, API, and SFTP) for financial reconciliation.
  • Ensure good data health: confirm sufficient information is passed on each request for strong data quality, and comply with AFT mandate requirements where applicable (optional).
  • Run a production smoke test:
    1. Process a real transaction. Buy a low-value live item using a real personal or corporate card. Do not use sandbox cards — they fail instantly in production.
    2. Verify the webhook response. Monitor your backend logs and confirm your system captures the live event and moves the order status from "Pending" to "Paid".
    3. Trigger a partial or full refund. Initiate a refund from your platform's backend or the Dashboard to confirm the reverse payment loop works end to end.
