FAQ

Answers to the questions merchants ask most during onboarding.

How long does onboarding take?

Timelines depend on your vertical, licensing, and how quickly documents are provided. Your onboarding team will share an estimate after the initial review.

Which documents do I need to provide?

See the Preparation page for the core checklist. Crypto (MCC 6051) and iGaming (MCC 7995) merchants have additional licensing requirements.

Can I test before going live?

Yes — you integrate and validate in sandbox first. Try the live Flow demo to see the payment experience.

Which integration should I choose?

Flow or the Hosted Payments Page are fastest to launch; the Payments API gives full control. See Payin for the options.

Do you support payouts to my customers?

Yes — via card payouts (AFT) and bank transfers. Coverage varies by region; see Payout.

Who do I contact during onboarding?

Your onboarding team is listed on the home page — hover a card to copy the email.
