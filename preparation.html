Preparation

Two things to get in place before you start building: your PCI DSS compliance and a sandbox account.

PCI DSS compliance

All merchants that accept payment cards are required to comply with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards (PCI DSS). You can review and validate your PCI DSS compliance with a Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ).

The type of SAQ you need to provide depends on your integration method.

  • SAQ A

    Flow, Hosted Payments Page (HPP), Payment Links, or our Mobile SDKs.

  • SAQ D

    Our Full Card API with your own integration platform.

Sandbox account creation

Set up a test account so you can build and validate before going live.

  1. 1

    Create a test account

    Use a company email address to create your test account at checkout.com/get-test-account.

  2. 2

    Check your email

    You will receive a welcome email from Okta with the activation link.

  3. 3

    Activate & set your password

    Activate your account and reset your password.

  4. 4

    Log in to the Dashboard

    Now you can log in to the Dashboard at dashboard.sandbox.checkout.com.

  5. 5

    Invite your colleagues

    Once you have activated and logged in as the Owner user, go to Settings → Roles and permissions and click + New User to invite more colleagues.
