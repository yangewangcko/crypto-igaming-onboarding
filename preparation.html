Preparation

Gather these before you apply — having them ready keeps onboarding fast.

What to prepare

The core information and documents we'll ask for.

  • Corporate documents

    Certificate of incorporation, ownership structure, and director / UBO identification.

  • Licensing

    Operating licences relevant to your jurisdiction and vertical.

  • Processing history

    Recent statements and monthly volumes, if you have prior processing.

  • Business & website details

    Live URL, business model, target markets, and settlement currency.

Vertical-specific requirements

Crypto — MCC 6051

VASP registration and proof of regulatory status where applicable to your operating markets.

iGaming — MCC 7995

Gambling licence(s) per operating market and your responsible-gaming policy.

Onboarding at a glance

  1. 1

    Apply & submit KYC / KYB

    Provide company details and complete Know-Your-Business verification.

  2. 2

    Risk & compliance review

    Underwriting assesses your business model, licensing, and MCC.

  3. 3

    Integration & testing

    Connect via API or Flow in sandbox, then validate test transactions.

  4. 4

    Go live

    Move to production credentials and start processing.
