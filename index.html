Onboarding steps

The end-to-end path from application to going live.

  1. 1

    Apply & submit KYC/KYB

    Provide company details and complete Know-Your-Business verification.

  2. 2

    Risk & compliance review

    Underwriting assesses your business model, licensing, and MCC.

  3. 3

    Integration & testing

    Connect via API or Flow in sandbox, then validate test transactions.

  4. 4

    Go live

    Move to production credentials and process live payments.

What to prepare

Have these ready to speed up onboarding.

  • Corporate documents

    Certificate of incorporation, ownership structure, director IDs.

  • Licensing

    Relevant operating licenses for your jurisdiction and vertical.

  • Processing history

    Recent statements and volumes, if you have a processing history.

Crypto (MCC 6051)

VASP registration and proof of regulatory status where applicable.

iGaming (MCC 7995)

Gambling license(s) per operating market and responsible-gaming policy.
